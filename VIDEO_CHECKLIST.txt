BACKUP PLAN ✓ (If Something Goes Wrong)
================================================================================

PROBLEM: Button doesn't work during recording
  FIX: Pause, refresh page (Ctrl+R), restart that section
       Edit out the pause later

PROBLEM: Server crashes
  FIX: Stop recording, restart server, verify it works, record again
       OR use pre-recorded backup footage (if you made one)

PROBLEM: You stumble on words multiple times
  FIX: Stop, take 5-minute break, restart recording
       Don't try to save a bad take

PROBLEM: Recording software freezes
  FIX: Force quit, restart software, verify settings, try again
       Consider using different software (e.g., Windows Game Bar)

PROBLEM: Audio has echo/background noise
  FIX: Move to quieter room, closer to mic, try again
       OR use noise reduction in post (Audacity, free)

PROBLEM: Video is over 4:05
  FIX: Cut Audit Trail section (save 20s)
       Speak slightly faster (but still clear)
       Trim pauses in editing

PROBLEM: Video is under 3:55
  FIX: Speak slightly slower (but natural)
       Add pauses after key statements
